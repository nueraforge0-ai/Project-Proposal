2.2 Concept of Literature Review

A literature review involves identifying, examining and analysing existing studies related to a particular research problem to establish what is already known, identify areas of uncertainty and determine what remains to be investigated. In this study, the literature review is important because various digital lost-and-found systems have already been developed to support the management and recovery of lost property. Examining and comparing these systems makes it possible to establish their contributions, identify their limitations and determine how the proposed system at Nkumba University will address the identified gaps.

Therefore, this review critically evaluates existing systems against the requirements of a university environment in which found property is received, registered and managed through a centralised custody point, rather than merely describing the systems individually.
