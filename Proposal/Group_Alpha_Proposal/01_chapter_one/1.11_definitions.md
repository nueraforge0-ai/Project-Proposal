### 1.11 Definition of Key Terms

- **Lost property:** property misplaced or forgotten by its owner and not stolen.

- **Found item:** property found by someone other than its owner and handed to the Security Unit.

- **Claim:** a request by a person who says an item is theirs, together with details that support the request.

- **Verification:** the check by a Security Officer that a claimant is the rightful owner before an item is released.

- **Unclaimed item:** a found item that nobody has claimed by the end of the retention period.

- **AI-assisted analytics:** machine-generated suggestions and summaries of system data, reviewed by authorised staff.

- **Match suggestion:** a ranked list of found items that may correspond to a lost-item report, shown to an officer for review.

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** a method of restricting system access to authorised users according to their roles.

- **Progressive Web Application (PWA):** a web application that can be installed on a device and used with limited connectivity.

- **Data minimisation:** the principle of collecting and keeping only the personal data that is necessary for a stated purpose.
