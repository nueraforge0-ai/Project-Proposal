### 1.3 Objectives

#### 1.3.1 General Objective

To design, develop and evaluate a web-based lost and found property management system with AI analytics that helps students and staff of Nkumba University to recover lost property, helps the Security Unit to register and release found items, and gives administrators summaries of lost and found activity.

#### 1.3.2 Specific Objectives

1.  To investigate how lost and found property is currently handled at Nkumba University, particularly at the main gate, and the challenges that owners and Security officers face, and to establish the functional, information and system requirements for the proposed system.

2.  To design and develop a web-based lost and found management system with role-based interfaces for users, Security Unit officers and Administrators, covering found-item registration, lost-item reporting and search, claim verification and release, and AI features for match suggestion, category suggestion and trend summaries.

3.  To test and evaluate the developed system in terms of functionality, usability, reliability, security and perceived usefulness.
