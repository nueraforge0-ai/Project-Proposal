### 1.4 Research Questions

- How is lost and found property currently handled at Nkumba University, what challenges do owners and Security officers face, and what functional, information and system requirements should a digital system meet?

- How can a web-based system allow users to report lost items and search found items, allow Security officers to register, verify and release found items, and allow Administrators to oversee activity, with access restricted by role?

- How can AI features support the matching of lost and found items, the categorising of registered items and the summarising of records, without replacing the decisions of Security officers?

- To what extent does the developed system meet user expectations in terms of functionality, usability, reliability, security and perceived usefulness?
