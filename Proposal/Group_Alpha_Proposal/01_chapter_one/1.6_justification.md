### 1.6 Justification

The project is justified by the need to move found property at Nkumba University from passive holding to active recovery. Students and staff gain a faster way to find out whether a lost item has been handed in, from a phone and without walking to the gate. Security Officers gain a register of what they hold, a verification step before release and a record of every release, which protects both officers and owners. Administrators gain summaries of what is lost, found and unclaimed, which can guide awareness messages and gate procedures. The project builds on infrastructure that already exists, the Security Unit (Nashonji, 2026) and hostel Wi-Fi (Nalwanga, 2024), and therefore complements rather than replaces current arrangements.

It is also a suitable computing problem. It combines web development, database design, authentication, role-based access control (Rangappa, 2023), file handling, information security, AI integration, usability and data protection in one practical system that real users on the campus can test.
