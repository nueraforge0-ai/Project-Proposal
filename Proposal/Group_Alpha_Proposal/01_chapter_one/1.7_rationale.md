### 1.7 Rationale

The rationale is to replace waiting with matching. At present, an item can sit at the gate for as long as it takes for its owner to think of asking. A searchable register lets owners rule out loss from their phones (Aremu et al., 2024), and match suggestions point officers to likely owners. Because officers already receive the items, the design starts with them, which is the gap left by systems that depend on finders posting items themselves (Tan & Chong, 2023; Murugiah & Mat Nawi, 2025). A PWA reflects widespread smartphone use and data-cost limits (Daily Monitor, 2026a; BrowserStack, 2026). A role-based structure keeps personal details away from people who do not need them (Rangappa, 2023). AI is used as an assistant for matching and summarising with human review, because model output can be inaccurate (Ji et al., 2023) and responsible use requires oversight (National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2023).

#### 1.7.1 Role of Artificial Intelligence in the Proposed System

The proposed system includes a limited Artificial Intelligence (AI) component to support Security Officers and Administrators. It will not replace officers and will not release any item. It will assist in three ways:

- Match suggestion: comparing a lost-item report with the register of found items and ranking the most likely matches for an officer to review.

- Category suggestion: proposing a category and key details when an officer registers a found item from a short description.

- Trend summaries: explaining database figures in plain language, for example which categories are lost most often and how long items stay unclaimed.

For example, if a student reports a lost item and a similar item was registered at the gate the same week, the system may show the record as a possible match. The officer then asks the claimant for details that only the owner would know before anything is released. Ownership is always confirmed by a person, never by the AI. The purpose of AI in the system is to save officers' time and help owners and items find each other sooner, while final responsibility for verification and release remains with the Security Unit.
