### 1.8 Expected Outcomes

- A documented analysis of current lost-and-found handling and its challenges at Nkumba University.

- A functional web-based prototype with progressive web application features for found-item registration, lost-item reporting and search.

- A claim, verification and release workflow with a record of every release.

- A user interface for students and staff, a Security Officer interface and an Administrator interface.

- Role-based authentication and access control protecting personal details.

- A structured database of items, reports, claims and statuses.

- AI-assisted match suggestion, category suggestion and trend summaries, labelled as AI output and reviewed by staff.

- Downloadable PDF and CSV reports and an anonymised dataset for future research.

- Evaluation results on functionality, usability, reliability, security and usefulness, with recommendations for improvement.
