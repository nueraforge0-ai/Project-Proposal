### 1.9 Key Assumptions

- The University Administration and the Security Unit will permit requirements gathering and prototype evaluation, including a trial of the registration and release workflow at the main gate with simulated items.

- Selected students, Security Officers and staff will give accurate information and honest feedback.

- Finders will continue to hand found items to the Security Unit at the main gate, and Security Officers will be willing to register those items in the system.

- Users have a smartphone or computer with a modern browser, and suitable internet or Wi-Fi is available for testing the main online functions.

- Access to an AI service will be available during development and evaluation **[CONFIRM: AI service and cost]**.

- The University Administration approves the research use of anonymised, aggregated data.

- Personal information will be handled according to university procedures and Uganda's data protection requirements (Uganda, 2019).

- Users understand that AI suggestions are suggestions only, and that ownership is confirmed by a Security Officer.
