### 2.10 Chapter Summary

This chapter reviewed how universities handle lost property, existing digital lost-and-found systems in Malaysia, Kenya and Uganda, verification of ownership, matching and AI, system security and data protection, and usability and mobile access. It found that digital systems are feasible and welcomed by users, but that the reviewed systems rely on users posting and handing over items themselves, rely on security questions for verification, and give limited attention to analytics for administrators. TAM and UTAUT were adopted as theoretical bases for the evaluation. The conceptual framework links the features of the proposed system and the intervening factors to improved recovery and management of lost property. The chapter supports the objectives and research questions in Chapter One and leads into the methodology in Chapter Three.
