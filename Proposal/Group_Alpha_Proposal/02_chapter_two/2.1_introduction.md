### 2.1 Introduction

This chapter reviews literature related to the design and implementation of a web-based lost and found property management system with AI analytics at Nkumba University. It is organised around the themes that arise from the objectives and research questions: how universities handle lost and found property, existing digital lost-and-found systems, verification of ownership and protection of personal items, matching and AI, system security and data protection, and usability and mobile access. The chapter then presents theoretical perspectives, an empirical review, a gap analysis, a statement of the unique contribution of this project, and a conceptual framework. Throughout, the system is understood as a tool that supports the Security Unit and does not replace its officers.
