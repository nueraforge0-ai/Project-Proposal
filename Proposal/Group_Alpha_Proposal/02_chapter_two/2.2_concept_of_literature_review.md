### 2.2 Concept of Literature Review

A literature review identifies, reads and analyses earlier work related to a research problem, so as to establish what is already known, what is uncertain and what remains to be done. For this study, the review matters because several digital lost-and-found systems already exist. Comparing them makes it possible to identify what each contributes, what each leaves out, and where the proposed Nkumba University system differs. The review therefore compares earlier systems against the requirements of a university where found property is held at a single custody point, and does not simply list them.
