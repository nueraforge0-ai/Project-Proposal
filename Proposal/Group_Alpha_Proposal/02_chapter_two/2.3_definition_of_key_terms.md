### 2.3 Definition of Key Terms

Lost-and-found management refers to the process through which information about missing or recovered property is recorded and communicated so that the property can be returned to its owner. A custody point is the place where found items are physically held, which at Nkumba University is the main gate. A web-based system is an internet-connected application through which users submit, search and manage records. AI-assisted analytics refers to the automated suggestion of matches and categories and the summarising of records, with output presented for human review. Role-based access refers to the restriction of functions and data to authorised users according to their roles. Claim verification refers to the process through which a Security Officer confirms that a claimant is the rightful owner before an item is released. These definitions are consistent with Chapter One and with the design decisions in Chapter Three.
