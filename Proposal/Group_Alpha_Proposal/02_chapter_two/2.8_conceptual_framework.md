### 2.8 Conceptual Framework

The framework shows the expected relationship between the features of the proposed system and improved recovery and management of lost property at Nkumba University.

| **Independent Variables** | **Intervening Factors** | **Dependent Variable** |
|----|----|----|
| Officer-led found-item registration at the main gate; Lost-item reporting and search; Claim verification and release; User authentication and role-based access; AI match suggestion and category suggestion; AI trend summaries and reports; Progressive web application access | Internet availability; User digital literacy; Willingness of finders to hand items to Security; Security Unit procedures; User acceptance and trust; Data-protection requirements | **Improved recovery and management of lost property**: faster discovery of found items; fewer unclaimed items; verified release; a record of every item; better information for administrators |

**Figure 2.1: Conceptual framework for the proposed web-based lost and found property management system with AI analytics at Nkumba University.**

The framework assumes that registration, search, claim verification, role-based access and AI-assisted functions will influence how well lost property is recovered and managed. Intervening factors such as connectivity, digital literacy, finders' willingness to hand items in, Security Unit procedures, user trust and data-protection requirements may affect how well the system works in practice. The dependent variable is assessed through the evaluation criteria in Chapter One: functionality, usability, reliability, security and perceived usefulness.
