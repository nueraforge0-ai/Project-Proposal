### 2.9 Synthesis of the Literature

The literature agrees that a searchable digital record helps owners and items find each other more quickly than posting and asking. The studies differ in emphasis. Some stress ease of posting and search (Tan & Chong, 2023), some matching and notification (Murugiah & Mat Nawi, 2025), and some AI-based matching of images and text (Ngu, 2025; Twayigize, 2025). This project does not aim to repeat any of them. It adopts what they have shown to work, namely structured records, photographs, search, notifications and matching, and applies it to a university where the Security Unit holds the items. It adds officer verification before release, analytics for administrators and safeguards for personal information (Uganda, 2019), which the reviewed systems describe only to a limited extent. The study also remains within the scope of a student project, with core functions first and AI features as enhancements.
