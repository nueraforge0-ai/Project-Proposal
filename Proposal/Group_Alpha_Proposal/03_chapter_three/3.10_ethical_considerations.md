### 3.10 Ethical Considerations

**Permission.** Permission will be sought from the University Administration and the Security Unit before requirements gathering and evaluation, and the work will follow the guidance of the supervisor and any approval procedure of the School of Computing and Informatics.

**Informed and voluntary participation.** Participants will be told the purpose of the study, what is required of them and how their information will be used. Participation is voluntary, consent will be obtained, and participants may withdraw at any time without penalty.

**Privacy and confidentiality.** Questionnaires will not ask for names. Interview and observation findings will be reported without identifying individuals. Data will be stored securely, used only for this study and deleted when no longer needed, in line with Uganda (2019).

**Handling of personal items and data.** The study will not collect real identity cards or other personal items. Test data will be simulated, and the AI service will receive no names, contacts or identity numbers.

**Integrity.** Data will be reported as collected. Because the researchers are students of the case-study institution, they will guard against bias and acknowledge all sources used.
