### 3.11 Anticipated Limitations

First, the sample is small and convenience sampling is used for students, so results may be affected by selection bias and cannot be generalised to all universities. The study will describe the sample clearly and treat the findings as specific to Nkumba University. Second, participants report their own views, which may be incomplete or favourable to the prototype. Anonymous questionnaires and a mix of methods will reduce this effect. Third, some participants may be unwilling to describe gate procedures, so the purpose and confidentiality arrangements will be explained.

Fourth, time, funds and access are limited, so the system will be a prototype tested with simulated data and not a fully deployed system. Its performance in long-term real use will not be known. Fifth, connectivity and device differences may affect testing, and the study will report the devices and conditions used. Finally, the AI matching test uses simulated items, so its results show how the method behaves in a controlled setting and not how it will behave on real items. Recommendations for further research will be made in the final report.
