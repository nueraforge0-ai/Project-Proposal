### 3.12 Chapter Summary

This chapter described a descriptive case study of Nkumba University using a mixed-methods approach. Data will be collected from a proposed sample of 58 respondents (50 students, 5 Security Officers and 3 ICT Directorate staff) through questionnaires, interviews, observation at the main gate and document review. The system will be built through iterative prototyping with Agile practices, designed with standard modelling techniques, and implemented as a responsive PWA using React, Express and MySQL with role-based access control and privacy safeguards. It will be tested and evaluated for functionality, security, reliability, AI matching quality, usability and perceived usefulness using simulated data. Quantitative data will be analysed with descriptive statistics and qualitative data with thematic analysis. The chapter also set out the ethical considerations and the limitations. The next chapter gives the preliminary system requirements and design.
