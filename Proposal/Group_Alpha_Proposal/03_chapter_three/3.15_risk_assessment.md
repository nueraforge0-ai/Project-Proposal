### 3.15 Risk Management

| **Risk** | **Likelihood** | **Impact** | **Mitigation** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Security Unit is unavailable for interviews, observation or testing | Medium | High | Ask for permission early through the supervisor; arrange fixed times; use simulated items if live testing is not possible |
| Low participation in questionnaires | Medium | Medium | Keep questionnaires short; collect them in person; use the pilot to remove unclear questions |
| AI suggests a wrong match | Medium | Medium | Officers review every suggestion; nothing is released without verification; the matching test measures accuracy before use |
| AI service cost or outage | Medium | Medium | Keep core functions independent of AI; set a spending limit; fall back to keyword search |
| Personal data exposed | Low | High | Role-based access, photographs outside public folders, personal data removed before AI calls, simulated data for testing, access log |
| Poor connectivity or power cuts during testing | High | Medium | PWA features, offline-tolerant forms, test at different times, keep local backups |
| Scope grows beyond the time available | Medium | High | Core features first; AI and export are enhancements; supervisor agrees the scope |
| A team member is unavailable because of other coursework | Medium | Medium | Shared tasks, version control, weekly meetings, a backup person for each lead role |
| Deployment or hosting fails before the demonstration | Low | High | Keep a working local copy and a recorded walkthrough as a fallback |
