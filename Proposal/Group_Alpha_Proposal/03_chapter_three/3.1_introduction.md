### 3.1 Introduction

This chapter describes the methods that will be used to design, develop and evaluate a web-based lost and found property management system with AI analytics for Nkumba University. It explains the research design and approach, the study area, the population, the sample and sampling techniques, the data collection methods, the system development methodology, the testing and evaluation procedures, the data analysis, the ethical considerations and the limitations of the study.

The methodology follows from the objectives in Chapter One and the literature in Chapter Two. Chapter One requires the study to investigate how lost and found property is handled at Nkumba University, to design and develop the system with its AI features, and to evaluate it. Chapter Two showed that the reviewed systems rely on users posting items, rely on security questions for verification and give limited attention to analytics. The methods below are chosen to establish the real situation at the main gate first, and then to build and test a system that fits it. Consistent with Chapter Two, the evaluation draws on the Technology Acceptance Model and the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology.
