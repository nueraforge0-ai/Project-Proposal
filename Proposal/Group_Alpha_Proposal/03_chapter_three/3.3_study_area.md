### 3.3 Study Area

The study will be conducted at Nkumba University's main campus on Nkumba Hill, Wakiso District, along the Kampala-Entebbe Highway. The university was selected because it has an established Security Unit that receives found property at the main gate, and because Wi-Fi masts have been installed in student hostels (Nalwanga, 2024). Prototype testing may include the main gate and selected hostels.
