### 3.4 Study Population

The target population is all Nkumba University students, the Security Officers who receive found items, and the ICT Directorate staff who would administer the system **[CONFIRM: office name]**. Because the study cannot reach all of them within the available time and resources, the accessible population is limited to students of the School of Computing and Informatics, Security Officers who agree to take part, and ICT Directorate staff who agree to take part. Students are included because they are the main owners and finders. Security Officers are included because they will register, verify and release items. Administrators are included because they will manage accounts and use the summaries.
