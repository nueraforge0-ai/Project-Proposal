### 3.5 Sample Size and Sampling Techniques

#### 3.5.1 Sample Size

The study proposes a sample of 58 respondents, shown in Table 3.2. The student sample is large enough to give useful descriptive results for a prototype study. The officer and administrator samples are small because these groups are small and each member holds specialised knowledge. The sample size will be confirmed with the supervisor and adjusted to the accessible population at the time of data collection.

**Table 3.2: Proposed sample**

| **Category** | **Sample** | **Sampling technique** | **Role in data collection and evaluation** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Students (School of Computing and Informatics) | 50 | Convenience sampling, aiming for a spread of years of study | Requirements questionnaire; prototype testing and evaluation questionnaire |
| Security Officers | 5 | Purposive sampling | Interviews; observation at the gate; testing of the officer interface; evaluation questionnaire |
| ICT Directorate staff | 3 | Purposive sampling | Interviews; testing of the administrator interface; evaluation questionnaire |
| **Total** | **58** |  |  |

#### 3.5.2 Sampling Techniques

Convenience sampling will be used for students because it gives quick, low-cost access to students who are available and willing. Its weakness is possible selection bias, so the study will try to include students from different years and will report this limitation. Purposive sampling will be used for Security Officers and ICT Directorate staff because they are chosen for their roles and knowledge of the process.
