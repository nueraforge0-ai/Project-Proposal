### 3.6 Data Collection Methods and Instruments

Data will be collected through questionnaires, interviews, direct observation and document review. Focus group discussions were considered and not adopted, because individual questionnaires and interviews are quicker to arrange for busy officers and students.

#### 3.6.1 Questionnaires

A structured questionnaire will be given to students at two stages. The first, at the requirements stage, will ask about year of study and device used, whether the student has lost or found property on campus, whether it was recovered or returned, how the student looked for it, what challenges were met, and which features the student would want. It will use closed questions with a few open-ended ones. The second, at the evaluation stage, is described in Section 3.8. The survey design of Tan and Chong (2023) will serve as a reference for the question topics, adapted to Nkumba University.

#### 3.6.2 Interviews

Semi-structured interviews will be held with Security Officers and ICT Directorate staff. The interview guide will cover how found items are received and stored, whether they are recorded, how owners are found, how claims are checked, how long unclaimed items are kept and what happens to them afterwards, and what access controls and oversight the university requires.

#### 3.6.3 Direct Observation

With the Security Unit's permission, the team will observe on several days how items are handed in and claimed at the main gate. Observation will record the steps followed and the information given, and will not record the names or details of the people involved.

#### 3.6.4 Document Review

Any existing Security Unit procedures, registers or forms and relevant university policies will be reviewed with permission. Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 will be reviewed to identify the data-protection requirements the system must follow. No personal details from existing records will be copied into the study.

#### 3.6.5 Pilot Testing, Validity and Reliability of Instruments

The supervisor will review the questionnaire and interview guide for clarity and fit with the objectives (content validity). They will then be tried with five students who are not part of the final sample, and unclear questions will be reworded. Likert-scale items will be checked for internal consistency with Cronbach's alpha once responses are entered.
