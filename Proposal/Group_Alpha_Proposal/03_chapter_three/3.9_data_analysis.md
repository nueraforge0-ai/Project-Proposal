### 3.9 Data Analysis

Quantitative data will be checked, coded and entered into Microsoft Excel or SPSS. They will be analysed with frequencies, percentages and mean scores, and presented in tables and charts. Mean scores on Likert items will be interpreted against the five evaluation criteria and the TAM and UTAUT constructs. Usability scores will be calculated by the System Usability Scale procedure. The AI matching test will be reported as the share of simulated lost reports for which the correct item appeared in the top three suggestions.

Qualitative data from interviews, observation notes, open-ended questions and document review will be analysed with thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2022). The team will read the notes, assign codes, group them into themes such as how owners find out, how claims are checked and how long items are kept, and use the themes to produce the requirements. Findings from the different sources will be compared to check whether they agree (triangulation).
