 2.3 Definition of Key Terms

**Lost-and-found management:** Refers to the process of recording, organising, tracking and communicating information about lost and recovered property to facilitate the identification of rightful owners and the return of recovered items.

**Custody point:** Refers to a designated location where recovered property is physically received, stored and managed until it is claimed by its rightful owner. In this study, the main gate Security Unit at Nkumba University is considered the proposed system's central point of custody, subject to confirmation during requirements gathering.

**Web-based system:** Refers to an application accessed through a web browser over the internet or an available network, enabling authorised users to access information and perform designated activities.

**AI-assisted analytics:** Refers to the use of artificial intelligence techniques to support the identification of potential matches between ownership claims and registered found items, recommend item categories and generate summaries of recorded lost-and-found activities, with all AI-generated outputs subject to human review.

**Role-based access control:** Refers to a security mechanism that assigns permissions to users according to their designated roles, thereby ensuring that each user can only access the functions and information authorised for that role.

**Claim verification:** Refers to the process through which authorised Security personnel assess the information and evidence provided by a person claiming an item to establish whether the individual is its rightful owner before the property is released.

The definitions provided in this section establish the meaning of key concepts as they are used throughout this study and guide the system requirements and design presented in subsequent chapters.
