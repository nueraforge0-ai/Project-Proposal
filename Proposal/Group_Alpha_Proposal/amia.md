3.12 Chapter Summary

This chapter presented the research methodology adopted for the study, which uses a descriptive case study design and a mixed-methods approach at Nkumba University. The study will involve a proposed sample of 58 respondents, comprising 50 students, 5 Security Officers and 3 members of the ICT Directorate, with data collected through questionnaires, interviews, observation and document review.

The proposed system will be developed using an iterative prototyping approach supported by Agile practices, with standard system modelling techniques applied during the design stage. Implementation will involve a responsive Progressive Web Application (PWA) using React, Express and MySQL, incorporating role-based access control and appropriate data protection measures.

The developed system will be tested and evaluated in terms of functionality, security, reliability, AI-assisted matching performance, usability and perceived usefulness, using simulated records where necessary to assess its operations without exposing actual personal information. Quantitative data will be analysed using descriptive statistical techniques, while qualitative data obtained from interviews and observations will be analysed thematically to identify common patterns and findings.

The chapter also discussed the ethical considerations and anticipated limitations associated with conducting the study and developing the proposed system. The next chapter presents the preliminary system requirements and design based on the findings and methodological procedures outlined in this chapter.
