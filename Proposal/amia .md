REVIEW 2.1 INTRODUCTION

This chapter reviews literature related to the design and implementation of a web-based Lost and Found Property Management System with AI Analytics at Nkumba University. The chapter is organised around key themes derived from the study objectives and research questions, including the management of lost and found property in universities, existing digital lost-and-found systems, ownership verification and protection of personal belongings, artificial intelligence and item-matching techniques, system security and data protection, and usability and mobile accessibility.

It further examines relevant theoretical perspectives, reviews empirical studies, identifies gaps in existing research, explains the unique contribution of the study, and presents the conceptual framework. Throughout the review, the proposed system is considered a supportive tool for the Security Unit, complementing rather than replacing the responsibilities of its personnel.
