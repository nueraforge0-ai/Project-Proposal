1.8 Expected Outcomes

The expected outcomes of this project include:

1. A documented analysis of the existing procedures for handling lost and found property at Nkumba University, including the challenges faced by students, staff and Security personnel.

2. A functional web-based prototype with Progressive Web Application (PWA) features that supports found-item registration by authorised Security personnel and allows students and staff to browse and search available found-item records.

3. A structured claim submission, ownership verification and property release workflow that maintains records of all completed releases.

4. Role-based user interfaces for students and staff, authorised Security personnel, and administrators, providing access to functionalities according to their respective responsibilities.

5. A secure authentication and role-based access control mechanism that restricts access to system functionalities and protects users' personal information from unauthorised access.

6. A structured database for storing found-item records, user accounts, ownership claims, verification details, release records and item statuses.

7. AI-assisted functionalities for suggesting potential matches between ownership claims and registered found items, recommending item categories during registration, and generating analytical summaries of lost-and-found trends, with all AI-generated suggestions subject to review by authorised personnel.

8. Downloadable administrative reports in PDF and CSV formats, together with anonymised data that may support future research and analysis, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

9. A documented evaluation of the developed system based on functionality, usability, reliability, security and user satisfaction, together with recommendations for future improvements.
